Amwali Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

What the app stores

Amwali stores your financial entries (income, expenses, goals, settings) on your own device. The developer does not run any server and does not receive your data.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests only the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (plus your email address to show which account is connected). It saves one file in the hidden application data folder of your own Google Drive. The app cannot see or change your other Drive files. The file is not encrypted by the app. Anyone who can access your Google account may be able to access it.

Optional AI assistant

If you add an API key for an AI provider that you choose, the text you send and the financial figures needed to answer are sent to that provider. This is off unless you set it up.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Sign out inside the app to stop syncing. To delete the Drive copy, open your Google Account, go to Data & privacy, then Third-party apps and services, choose Amwali, and delete its hidden app data. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on the device.
